# HDR Forge

**Professional HDR Image Processing for Mac**

Transform your bracketed photo sets into stunning High Dynamic Range images with Metal-accelerated performance.

---

## Features

- **Batch Processing** — Process hundreds of bracket sets in a single operation
- **Intelligent Detection** — Auto-groups photos by exposure, timestamp, and filename
- **Metal Acceleration** — Native GPU compute for blazing fast performance on Apple Silicon
- **Professional Output** — Export to JPEG, HEIF, TIFF, OpenEXR, Radiance HDR, and DNG
- **Real-Time Preview** — See adjustments instantly with histogram and clipping overlay
- **Ghost Detection** — Advanced algorithms handle moving subjects in handheld shots

## System Requirements

- macOS 15.0 (Sequoia) or later
- Metal-compatible GPU
- 8GB RAM minimum (16GB recommended)

## Supported Formats

**Input:** CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, DNG, ORF, RW2, JPEG, HEIF, TIFF, PNG, OpenEXR, Radiance HDR

**Output:** JPEG, HEIF (with HDR gain map), 16-bit TIFF, 32-bit Linear TIFF, OpenEXR, Radiance HDR, DNG

## Getting Started

1. **Import** — Drag a folder of bracketed photos into HDR Forge
2. **Select** — Choose a bracket set from the left panel
3. **Adjust** — Fine-tune with presets or manual controls
4. **Export** — Save your HDR image in your preferred format

### Common Questions

**Q: How many images should be in a bracket set?**
A: HDR Forge works best with 3, 5, or 7 bracketed exposures. The app automatically detects and groups your brackets.

**Q: Why are my images not being grouped together?**
A: Ensure your images have EXIF data with exposure information, or use consistent filename patterns (e.g., IMG_001_-2EV, IMG_001_0EV, IMG_001_+2EV).

**Q: What's the difference between Free and Pro?**
A: The free tier includes full HDR processing and preview, with JPEG export at 50% resolution. Pro ($9.99, one-time) unlocks full resolution export in all formats.

**Q: Can I process RAW files?**
A: Yes! HDR Forge supports all major RAW formats via Apple's native RAW engine.
